About

"Compound interest is the eighth wonder of the world. He who understands it, earns it ... he who doesn't ... pays it." - Albert Einstein

The Compound Interest Tool is a clean, mobile-friendly compound interest calculator designed to help you understand how savings and investments can grow over time. It combines a future value calculator with an investment growth calculator so you can model both a starting balance and optional monthly contributions.

Adjust the interest rate, compounding frequency, and time horizon to see how compounding interest affects your total balance. This savings calculator updates instantly, making it easy to compare scenarios and plan for goals like emergency funds, down payments, or long-term retirement savings.

We built this interest rate calculator to stay simple, fast, and accessible on any device. For deeper planning, use the results as a starting point and consult a qualified financial professional.
